## Residual Note (PASS_BUT_RESIDUAL — observed 2.34× predicted)

The falsifiable prediction was ~415% USD-APR (4.15 fraction). Observed p5 is 9.71 (970%).

The predicted calculation used `sigma_others_long ≈ 75 YU` (effective pool from empirical reverse-engineering). The engine reads `sigma_others` from the raw `order_book_raw` parquet CLO-range sum, which yields ~165 YU (includes orders at tick 0.7% that are in-range for the CLO bounds [9300, 9500]). If the effective pool is 75 YU (Pendle's weighting excludes some orders), our share is higher than expected.

This discrepancy does **not** block halt-lift: the strategy is profitable in either interpretation. Bug #6 hypothesis (effective-pool weighting) is queued as non-blocking investigation for Phase 6.

The PASS_BUT_RESIDUAL label reflects the plan's classification ladder where "residual" means any PASS outside the 50%-band around the point prediction. It does NOT imply the verdict is weaker than predicted.
