## Key Findings

**T1 — Formula source:** The shape `share = q_me / (sigma_others + q_me) × side_split × budget` is **lexically endorsed** by Pendle's V2 Incentives docs ("time and notional value weighted basis") and **structurally confirmed** by the SDK type `MakerIncentiveSimulationResponse.provideLiquidityIncentiveReward`. **However**, the actual `provideLiquidityIncentiveReward` share calculation runs on Pendle's backend; the SDK fetches only the final accrued amount as a Merkle leaf, and the on-chain `MultiTokenMerkleDistributor.sol` is agnostic. Verified-with-caveat. Critical second-order finding: two distinct maker reward streams (`filledVolumeIncentiveReward` vs `provideLiquidityIncentiveReward`) — the bot's strategy targets the latter.

**T2 — Dapp 489% reconciliation:** Dapp's 489% LONG APR badge reproduces from `(q_me=5 YU, sigma=165 YU, side_split=0.760)` → 486.4% APR, within ±0.5%. CONTEXT.md's "effective pool ≈ 75 YU" assumption produces 1018% APR (matches W5 raw-engine 970.6% → confirms W5 overshoot was driven by wrong sigma, not wrong formula shape). RETRACTED.

**Discovered: 165 vs 329 sigma gap.** Dapp's "While Active/day" estimator uses an implicit `sigma ≈ 329 YU` (≈ 2× the headline-badge anchor of 165). The factor-of-2 reflects an unresolved Pendle backend question: is `sigma_others` raw orderbook in CLO range (165), or the broader population of currently-or-near-eligible orders (329)? Three sigma anchors retained for the band: `{75, 165, 329}` YU.
