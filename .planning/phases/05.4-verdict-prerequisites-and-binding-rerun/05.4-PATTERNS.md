### `python/src/boros_brain/fill_model/fit.py` + `refit_loop.py` — Bug C look-ahead bound

**Current bug location** — `fill_model/refit_loop.py:42-44`:


In production live mode, `chain_time.now_cached()` ≈ now; in backtest, the engine reads parquet covering 25 days but the fitter still sees `now()` ≈ 2026-05-04 — fitting on data 25–30 days AFTER the replay tick. Material 30× p_fill underestimate per CONTEXT.md.

**Recommended fix pattern** (mirrors `_calibration_at_chain_ts`):

```python

    config: BotConfig,
    run_id: str,
```
